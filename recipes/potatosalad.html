Potato Salad

Description

This warm German potato salad features tender potatoes tossed in a flavorful and tangy bacon vinaigrette. Pairs perfectly with grilled sausages or schnitzel and is always a crowd-pleaser at potlucks.

Unlike mayonnaise-based American potato salads, German potato salad is tossed in a vinegar-based dressing made with bacon drippings.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups diced peeled potatoes
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • ¼ cup white vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Place potatoes in a large pot and add water to cover by at least 1 to 2 inches; bring to a boil
  2. Cook onion in the bacon grease over medium heat until browned
  3. Transfer warm potato salad to a serving dish
